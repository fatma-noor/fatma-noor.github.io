Feature Selection for High-Dimensional Neural Decoding

Compared six feature selection methods (Forward Stepwise, mRMR, Lasso, Elastic Net, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting importance) on a neuroscience dataset where features vastly outnumbered samples (11,190 features, 683 trials). Built a unified evaluation pipeline scoring balanced accuracy, sparsity, and runtime. Forward Stepwise won the trade-off: 0.774 balanced accuracy with just 30 features.

Distributed CTR Prediction at Scale

Built a PySpark pipeline on Google Cloud Dataproc to predict click-through rates on the Avazu dataset. Compared a per-channel local logistic regression against a Spark MLlib global baseline to test whether partitioning was worth the added complexity. Size-up experiments across 25 to 100 percent of the data showed 3.6x scaling on the channel-partitioned model at 4x data.

Sephora Product Exclusivity Analysis

Analysed Sephora's exclusivity strategy across 8,494 products and around 1 million reviews. Built regression models, ran sentiment analysis on review text, and clustered products by engagement profile. Found that exclusivity drives engagement (loves, reviews) but not stronger sentiment, so it works as an attention strategy.
